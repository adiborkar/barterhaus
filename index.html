Chapter 04 · The credit system

You don't have to
trade directly.

Classic barter fails because you need someone who wants exactly what you offer, right now. Haus credits break that deadlock. Help someone today — spend with someone else entirely, next month.

Rule 01

1 credit = 1 meaningful service.

An hour of teaching, a delivered logo, a photography session. You and the other person agree on what's fair. Community guidelines keep it honest.

Rule 02

Credits never expire.

No pressure to spend within a window. The Haus runs on goodwill and patience, not deadlines. Give when you can, receive when you need.

Rule 03

No cash out. Ever.

Credits are community tokens. They exist only here, in the Haus. The moment we attach a cash value, it becomes work. That's not what this is.

Chapter 05 · The Haus promise

What we will
never do.

Product principles are easier to defend than product features. These are ours. Six lines in the sand.

Promise 01
Show cash equivalents.
Never

We will never display "your hour is worth $X". The moment we do, this becomes a job. Credits are credits — a different unit entirely.

Promise 02
Let credits leave.
Never

No cash out. No transfer to external wallets. Credits circulate within this community and nowhere else. That's what makes them mean something different.

Promise 03
Punish bad reviews.
Never

This isn't your livelihood. A low rating here doesn't threaten your rent. Do good work because you care, not because an algorithm penalises you.

Promise 04
Leave you alone.
Never, if disputes hit

If something goes wrong, our team steps in. We investigate, we listen, we compensate the affected party in credits. The community is protected.

Promise 05
Forget local.
Never

We're in Singapore first because we want to build real relationships, not just user counts. The Haus stays rooted in community.

Promise 06
Price your passion.
Never, that's yours

You define the terms. You set the unit. You decide when you offer and when you step back. Your craft, your pace, your rules.
