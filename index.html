Chapter 01. How it works.

Simple enough to explain
at a dinner table.

Three steps. No jargon. If it takes more than a minute to describe, we haven't built it right.

Step 01
List what you offer.
Your craft, your unit, your terms.
01 of 03
A piano lesson. A brand logo.

Offer by the hour or by the deliverable. A photography session, a poem, a tuned guitar. Your craft, your rules.

Step 02
Earn Haus credits.
Plus three bonus credits at signup.
02 of 03
One service. One credit.

Complete a service, earn a credit. Every new member starts with three bonus credits. Enough to receive before you give. Credits never expire.

Step 03
Spend on what you need.
No direct trade required.
03 of 03
Browse. Spend. Receive.

Spend your credits on someone else's skill. You never have to trade directly. Value flows freely across the community, in whatever order it wants.

Chapter 02. The tension.

There are only
three options.

When your craft means something, three doors appear. Two of them close. The third is the reason we're building this.

Option I
Turn it into
a business.
Admin. KPIs. Midnight emails.
01 of 03
The hustle route.

You start charging. Admin kicks in. Clients email at midnight. What you loved becomes a KPI, and the joy erodes quietly until it's gone.

Joy erodes.
Option II
Do it
for free.
Generous. Invisible. Unsustainable.
02 of 03
The gift route.

Generous, but unsustainable. It feels invisible. You gave your time, your craft, your energy, and walked away with nothing tangible.

Feels undervalued.
Option III
Barterhaus.
Credits. Community. Circulation.
03 of 03
The third way.

Your craft earns credits. Credits unlock what you need. Value circulates through the community, without a price tag and without the pressure.

A different unit entirely.

Chapter 04. The credit system.

You don't have to
trade directly.

Classic barter fails because you need someone who wants what you offer, right now. Haus credits break that deadlock.

Rule 01.

One credit. One meaningful service.

An hour of teaching, a delivered logo, a photography session. Community guidelines keep it honest.

Rule 02.

Credits never expire.

No pressure to spend within a window. Give when you can, receive when you need.

Rule 03.

No cash out. Ever.

Credits are community tokens. The moment we attach a cash value, it becomes work. That's not what this is.

Chapter 05. The Haus promise.

What we will
never do.

Product principles are easier to defend than product features. These are ours. Six lines in the sand.

Promises 01 + 02
Never show cash equivalents.
Credits never leave the Haus.
01 + 02 of 06
Never a price tag.

We will never display "your hour is worth $X". The moment we do, this becomes a job. Credits are a different unit entirely, and they stay that way.

Promise 02. No cash out. No external transfers. Credits circulate inside the Haus, nowhere else.

Promises 03 + 04
Never punish
for reviews.
Disputes always resolved.
03 + 04 of 06
No algorithmic rent.

This isn't your livelihood. A low rating here doesn't threaten your rent. Do good work because you care, not because an algorithm penalises you.

Promise 04. If something goes wrong, our team steps in, listens, and compensates the affected party in credits.

Promises 05 + 06
Never forget local.
Never price your passion.
05 + 06 of 06
Small, rooted, honest.

Singapore first because we want real relationships, not user counts. The Haus stays rooted in community.

Promise 06. You define the terms, the unit, the pace. Your craft, your rules. We never price your passion.
